Wrap-up

Five things to take away

01 Evaluation covers reasoning, behaviour and operational outcomes.

02 Trace inspection is central to debugging agents.

03 Autonomy brings its own safety and security risks.

04 Reliability patterns are what make an agent production-grade.

05 Human oversight stays critical in high-impact systems.

These are the five conclusions from the session agenda, in the same order as the blocks. Ask each learner to name the one pattern they would add to their own project first. Common good answers: an oracle-based golden set, the refund-style policy check on side-effecting tools, idempotency keys.
