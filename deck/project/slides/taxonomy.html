Failure taxonomy

Five failures, each with its own signature in the trace

FailureWhat the trace showsFix it in
ReasoningRight data retrieved, wrong conclusion drawnPrompt, policy in code
PlanningSteps skipped or out of order: acts before verifyingPlan checks, preconditions
Infinite loopSame call, same args, again and againLoop guard, step budget
Tool misuseWrong args, units or tool: 12900 for $129Schemas, arg validation
Silent failureEvery step green, outcome wrong, reply claims successCheck results, oracle

Notebook 02 builds one detector per row and runs them over every trace.

Silent failures are the most dangerous: no error, no alert, a happy customer message, and no money moved. Loops are the most expensive. Ask learners which of the five their own past projects have hit. In the notebook, diagnose() runs the detectors in cause order, so the earliest cause wins when a trace shows more than one symptom.
