Block 1 · Introduce evaluation · Notebook 01

Score the world the agent leaves behind, not the story it tells.

An agent's reply is its own account of what it did. The ledger, the outbox and the human queue are what it actually did.

This is the thesis of the evaluation block. In the first demo cell we run T01 and print both the agent's reply and the payments ledger. Every oracle in the lab reads the state of the world rather than the agent's words. The LLM judge comes in only for what language alone can judge: tone, clarity, honesty.
